# Mederi Trademark Policy & Guidelines

This document outlines the trademark policy and brand guidelines for the **Mederi** project.

The goal of this policy is to encourage widespread use, adoption, and contribution to Mederi while preserving the integrity of the Mederi brand, ensuring users know whether they are using the official software, and protecting against consumer confusion in the software and AI agent ecosystem.

---

## 1. Trademarks Covered

The trademarks covered by this policy (collectively, the **"Mederi Marks"**) include:
- The word mark **Mederi™** and **Mederi** (in software, developer tools, libraries, frameworks, AI agent applications, and related services)
- The official Mederi logos, icons, and visual identifiers
- Any stylization or combination thereof used to identify the official Mederi project

The Mederi Marks are trademarks of **lzdev42** in the United States and other countries.

## 2. Relationship with the Open Source License

Mederi’s source code is released under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**. 

Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 explicitly states:
> *"This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the Work and reproducing the content of the NOTICE file."*

**Code Freedom vs. Trademark Protection:**
- You are free to read, use, modify, and distribute the source code and binaries subject to the Apache 2.0 license.
- The open source license does **not** grant you permission to use the Mederi Marks to distribute modified versions, brand your own commercial products, or imply endorsement or official affiliation.

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Prior Written Permission Needed)

You may use the Mederi Marks without prior written consent in the following situations:

### 3.1 Truthful Factual Statements & Compatibility (Nominative Fair Use)
You may use the Mederi name to accurately describe compatibility, interoperation, or relationships with your own software, provided that:
- Your product name does not begin with or incorporate "Mederi" as a primary brand element.
- The phrasing makes it clear that your product is independent.
  - **Acceptable**: *"AwesomeTool: A plugin for Mederi™"*, *"Client library compatible with Mederi™"*, *"Built on top of the Mederi™ framework"*.
  - **Unacceptable**: *"Mederi Pro"*, *"Mederi Assistant"*, *"Official Mederi Studio"*.

### 3.2 Unmodified Distributions
You may distribute verbatim, unmodified copies of official Mederi releases (source or compiled binaries) under the name Mederi, provided the original copyright and trademark notices remain intact.

### 3.3 Community, Educational, and Commentary Use
You may use the Mederi name in articles, tutorials, conference talks, books, and discussions regarding the software, as long as it does not suggest official sponsorship or partnership by lzdev42.

---

## 4. Uses Requiring Renaming or Explicit Permission

To protect users from confusion, the following uses are **strictly restricted**:

### 4.1 Modified Versions and Forks
If you create a fork, produce a custom build with code modifications, or bundle derivative works:
- **You must choose a new, distinct name and logo** for your software.
- You must not distribute your modified binary or service under the name "Mederi".
- You may include a truthful statement in your documentation or repository, such as:
  > *"This project is forked from / based on Mederi™ (originally by lzdev42)."*

### 4.2 False Affiliation and Endorsement
You may not use the Mederi Marks in any manner that falsely implies:
- Official endorsement, sponsorship, or certification by lzdev42;
- That your product, organization, or service is the official maintainer of Mederi.

### 4.3 Domain Names, Social Handles, and Organizations
You may not register or use domain names, package names, social media handles, or company/organization names that consist primarily of "Mederi" or confusingly similar variations, unless specifically authorized in writing.

---

## 5. Trademark Attribution & Notice Rules

When you refer to Mederi in public documentation, websites, or promotional materials:
1. **Use the ™ Symbol**: Include the `™` symbol on first or most prominent mention (e.g., `Mederi™`).
2. **Attribution Statement**: Include the following notice in the colophon, footer, or legal notices section:
   > *"Mederi™ and the Mederi logo are trademarks of lzdev42 in the United States and other countries."*
3. **Do Not Dilute**: Do not use "Mederi" as a generic noun or a verb. Use it as a proper adjective (e.g., *"the Mederi™ framework"*).

If you have questions about this policy, or wish to request permission for a use not covered above, please open an issue or discussion on the official GitHub repository.
